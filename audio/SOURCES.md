## Mix limitations
Preview mix is waveform/timing based, not auditioned. Two engine revs are gain swells over a recorded Ferrari, not independently identified recorded throttle revs. No fabricated music or synthesized sound effect is represented as a field recording. Diegetic sound shares the scene world-time slowdowns, while the original music remains continuous and unretimed. NYPD-specific siren authenticity, a dramatic silence before the actual drop, final loudness, and perceptual timing require supervisor review.
